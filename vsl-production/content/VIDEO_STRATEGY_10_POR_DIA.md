## VARIAÇÕES DE TEMPLATE (rodar em ciclos de 7 dias)

```
[0-2s] Hook visual: closeup palma com luz dourada
[2-8s] "This line here... means you've been doing this in love"

[20-28s] "And that's why [consequência emocional]"

```

```
[0-3s] Hook: "She came to me after 3 failed relationships..."

[15-25s] "Her palm showed exactly why"
[25-35s] Revelação do padrão
[35-45s] "Your reading takes 3 minutes. Link in bio."

```

### Template D — "POV / Trend"
```
[0-2s] "POV: You finally understand why you keep attracting [type]"

### Template E — "Before/After"
```

[15-25s] Explicar o que mudou (o padrão identificado)

[0-3s] "Tell me your main concern in love and I'll tell you what your palm says"
[3-20s] Responder 3 comentários comuns (montar os próprios)
